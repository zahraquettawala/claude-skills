# claude-skills

Skills for [Claude](https://claude.ai). Each folder is one skill: a `SKILL.md` file with instructions Claude follows.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [responding-to-my-texts](responding-to-my-texts/SKILL.md) | Reads your text threads in Messages on your Mac and replies in *your* texting voice, matching the tone you already use with each person. Two modes: **one by one** (you approve each draft before it sends) or **mass respond** (you pick who to reply to once, and Claude replies to all of them hands-off). Includes a triage mode for when you're buried in unread texts, plus an optional "(this message was generated by AI)" tagline. |
| [responding-to-linkedin](responding-to-linkedin/SKILL.md) | Reads your LinkedIn messages in the Claude desktop app's built-in browser and replies in *your* professional voice, matching how you already write to each person. One by one (you approve each reply) or mass respond (you pick who once). Summarizes recruiter roles, asks if you're interested, and can pull your resume and blurb when you are. |

## Install a skill

1. Download this repo (**Code → Download ZIP**) and unzip it.
2. Zip just the skill's folder (for example `responding-to-my-texts/`, with `SKILL.md` inside).
3. In Claude, go to **Customize → Skills** and upload the ZIP.
4. Turn the skill on, then ask Claude something like "help me respond to my texts".

### Requirements for responding-to-linkedin

- The Claude desktop app with its built-in browser, signed in to LinkedIn

### Requirements for responding-to-my-texts

- A Mac with the Messages app signed in (iMessage synced from your iPhone)
- The Claude desktop app with **Computer use** turned on (Settings → Desktop app → Computer use)

Without computer use, you can still paste a thread or screenshot into the chat and the skill will help you draft a reply.

### Using responding-to-my-texts from your phone

On your phone, the skill defaults to **phone mode**: send a screenshot of the thread and it writes each bubble ready to copy and send. iOS doesn't let apps read or send iMessages, so you send it yourself.

To have it send for you while you're on your phone, say "use my Mac". Your Mac then does the reading and sending, as long as it's reachable:

- Keep the Mac awake on power: **System Settings → Battery → Options → "Prevent automatic sleeping on power adapter when the display is off"**. Or use a keep-awake app like Amphetamine.
- Keep the **Claude desktop app** open, and add it under **System Settings → General → Login Items** so it reopens after a restart.
- Keep **Computer use** turned on in the Claude desktop app.

If the Mac can't be reached, the skill falls back to phone mode.

## Privacy

The skill only opens the threads you ask about (or the recent threads it scans when you choose mass respond), never sends anything without your OK, and doesn't copy your messages anywhere else. In one-by-one mode you approve every message. In mass-respond mode, picking who to reply to is your OK for that batch.
